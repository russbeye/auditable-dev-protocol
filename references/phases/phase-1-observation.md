## Phase 1: Observation

**Do:** Restate the problem in your own words before any planning. The restatement must cover three
things:

- What the problem is.
- What the problem is not.
- What success looks like at a human level, not at a test level.

Create `.adp/<task.id>/audit-log.md` now, as *The artifact chain* says. Write the Problem Statement
into the audit log as its first entry. Open mission control on the audit log, and give the one-line
notice that *the observability layer* gives. Every later artifact appends to the same file, live and
in full.

**Gate:** The developer must confirm the restatement. Loop on corrections until the developer
confirms it. Then treat the restatement as locked. It does not change without a logged reason.
