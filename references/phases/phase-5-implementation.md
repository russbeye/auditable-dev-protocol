## Phase 5: Implementation

**Do:** Write the code. Annotate every non-trivial decision in the Decision Log as you make it. Do
not save entries up for PR time. Log a decision made under uncertainty immediately, with its
confidence level. The Decision Log grows here and stays open through Phase 8.

**Gate:** "Done" is not "compiles" or "tests pass." Done means that every decision in the
implementation has a corresponding Decision Log entry.
